      BOOT CAMP

      How to play

      HOW TO WIN

      First to 10 kills.

      • Free-for-All: the first player to 10 kills wins.
      • 2 vs 2: the first team to 10 kills wins. No friendly fire.
      • Every tank has 10 health (five pips). A shell does 1 damage, a laser 4.
      • Destroyed tanks respawn after 3 seconds, protected for 3 more.
      • After a win, everyone presses Rematch within 20 seconds, or Leave Room.

      PC CONTROLS

      Drive, point, click.

      • W A S D drive. Hold two keys to go diagonally.
      • Your mouse aims the turret, separately from driving.
      • Hold left click to keep firing, one shell every 0.42 seconds.
      • The dashed line shows where your shot heads.
      • Joining goes full screen. Esc exits.

      PHONE & TABLET

      Two thumbs. Full control.

      • Left stick: drag to drive.
      • Right stick: drag to aim. You keep firing until you let go.
      • Menu (top right): Full Map, sound, invite and Leave Room.
      • Turn your phone sideways for a wider battlefield.

      FIND YOUR TANK

      Look for the glowing ring.

      • Your tank wears a thick glowing ring in your color.
      • Your name tag ends with a ★.
      • Your score card has a colored edge.

      GETTING HIT

      Feel the heat.

      • When you're hit, flames flare around the screen edges and the screen shakes.
      • −1 or −4 pops up above any tank that takes damage.
      • Android phones buzz on every hit.
      • At 3 health or less the edges keep smouldering. Find cover or a Restore heart.

      BOUNCING BULLETS

      Bank shots around cover.

      • Shells bounce off walls and edges up to 6 times.
      • Bounce shots to hit tanks hiding behind cover.
      • Your own shells never hurt you.
      • Enemy shells that touch yours destroy each other.
      • The room creator can turn bouncing off.

      POWER-UPS

      Drive over a token.

      • A token appears every 12 seconds (two at most) and vanishes after 20.
      • Timed powers last 10 seconds. The top-left badge counts down.
      • A new power replaces your old one; dying ends it.
      • The room creator can turn powers off.
